# Flyer restyle of both FranklinWH routes (task 45272822)

Task https://admin.gomega.ai/tasks/45272822-6582-4ec7-9498-18bef55b6c58. Client direction from Rachel Raak,
Nov 23 2026: restyle `/franklinwh` and `/franklinwh-lower-peninsula` to follow Peninsula Solar's six-panel
direct-mail flyer (`Peninsula_Intro_1_of_6.png` to `6_of_6.png`): deep navy, cream text, gold accents, an
elegant serif, the "Own Your Energy" badge and a dark evening hero. Visual edit only.

## What this supersedes, and what it does not

**Superseded on the two campaign routes and the shared `/privacy` chrome:** the light FranklinWH palette
(orange `#D38A3D`, white, `#F5F7F5`, black text) and the Inter-only type direction from the original build
(task 6175fe7a) and its `task-spec.json` `brand` block. `task-spec.json` now records the flyer palette and
keeps the old values under `brand.supersededColors`.

**Not superseded (unchanged):** every word of copy, including the six human send-back strings (task
3d9afce8), error/success/alert text, disclaimers and the responsive CTA labels; section order and the seven
anchors; both forms (six fields `name, phone, email, utility, offGrid, message`, only name and phone
required, validate-first direct send); each route's phone and `tel:` href; GTM, MegaTag, CTM and every
conversion event; the privacy policy text and links; the client photo withdrawal (Nov 19: no Arnold
photos, no Peninsula solar-panel photos); the protected root `/` and its assets.

`/privacy` shares the route group's stylesheet, header and footer, so it inherits the navy theme, the badge
and the serif headings. Its text and links are unchanged; this is a theme change, not a policy edit.

Role aliases `paper`, `surface`, `ink`, `muted` and `navy` (the interactive accent) point at these values so
the field, checkbox, success-panel and privacy components, which were outside this task's file scope, take
the palette without edits. Fonts: Cormorant Garamond (variable, upright and italic) for h1-h3 and display
lines, Inter for body, both via `next/font/google`. Lining figures are set page-wide because Cormorant's
default old-style "1" reads as "I".

## Section treatments

- Header: the badge (72px, 88px from lg) with a gold rule and the reversed FranklinWH mark from 640px.
- Hero: italic cream h1 on an evening gradient; the existing audience line set as the gold italic subhead;
  the manufacturer aPower S photo toned to evening (a light CSS grade plus a radial navy multiply that is
  heavy at the edges and light over the units, so they stay near-white) with its top edge masked into the
  sky above it. The photo keeps its 16:10 crop, column width, `sizes` and srcset, so
  its delivered density is unchanged. The copy sits in the sky above the photo, as on flyer panel 1, rather
  than on the photo's pixels: the controller fills the photo's upper half, so text laid over it would cover
  the product. It is the manufacturer's photo and is not captioned as a Peninsula installation.
- Backup benefits: gold serif row titles between gold hairlines; the aGate render in a navy glow panel,
  its transparent bottom padding cropped by CSS (`object-top`).
- Product family: the four models as the flyer panel 3 ruled two-track rows (serif name, sans role). The
  caption is the gold italic subhead. No specs were added. The manufacturer renders carry a faint floor shadow drawn for white pages that shows as a hard-edged
  light band on navy; `sections/RenderFloorFilter.tsx` (an inline SVG alpha threshold) removes it in the
  browser, so the image files stay untouched.
- In-house process: centered title with a gold rule over a ruled three-up (panel 2), numbered because it is
  a real sequence.
- Reviews and FAQ: centered titles with gold rules; reviews as italic serif quotes.
- Final quote: the panel 6 bordered callout (1px gold border, harbor fill, centered title and gold rule),
  content-sized.
- Footer: centered, closing on the badge, like panel 6.

## Tests changed

- `contrast.test.ts`: rewritten for the new palette. It now checks cream, mist and gold text on every ground,
  including the composited glow and hero-sky peaks; navy on gold, gold-light and the cream press state;
  pending, alert, input, disabled, checkbox and form-card pairs (through the role aliases); 3:1 for
  control borders, the coral error border and gold focus outlines; and that cream is never set on the gold fill. More pairs than before.
- `layoutContract.test.ts` header test: the installer-mark assertion now pins the badge (`src`, 192x191,
  `h-[72px]`) instead of the 480x57 wordmark at `h-[22px]`. The fixed-height, auto-width and `shrink-0` checks
  are unchanged.
- `task-spec.json` `brand`: the flyer palette (see above), so `provenance.test.ts` keeps checking that every
  spec color exists in the stylesheet. That assertion is unchanged.

No form, validation, tracking, byte-preservation or image-density assertion was changed.
